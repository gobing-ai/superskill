### Decisions so far

- **Guide triage (2026-08-13)** — of 10 claims: 3 absorbed (progressive disclosure, staleness,
  contradiction-finding), 1 exposed a live rubric bug, 4 already covered by `noOpDensity` /
  `duplicationRatio` / `refine --kind split` / §6.0 of the constitution, 2 rejected.
- **Root finding (2026-08-13)** — `completeness` (weight 0.25, `quality/magent.ts:26-39`) counts
  `##` headings **in one file body**; `conciseness` (weight 0.15, `scoreLength(body, 1000, 8000)`)
  is outweighed. The scoring gradient points at one ~8000-char file with six headings — the exact
  ball of mud the guide warns about, and the opposite of what `refine --kind split` recommends.
- **Corroboration (2026-08-13)** — `templates/magent/default.md` is 14,101 chars, scoring ≈ 0.24 on
  `scoreLength(_, 1000, 8000)`. Our scaffolder emits a config our own scorer marks down.
- **Link resolution is the staleness check (2026-08-13)** — `quality/magent.ts` imports no
  filesystem API, so it cannot distinguish a live link from a dangling one. Threading `basePath`
  delivers the guide's staleness claim as a side effect; no separate mechanism needed.
- **Re-grade policy (2026-08-13, operator)** — link-resolved governance areas earn **full credit**,
  equal to an inline section. Additive only. Rejected the inline-length cap, which would regress
  existing stored evaluations.
- **`contradiction` lands once, not five times (2026-08-13)** — superseded by the premise
  correction below. The tag still lands once in the CLI, but the doc surfaces do not inherit it.
- **The `99` constitution is already ahead of the guide (2026-08-13)** — §6.0 rule 2 ("a fact lives
